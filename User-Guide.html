Get started

Enable GGA Casting Assistant and libWrapper in your GURPS Game Aid world. Select your character’s token, then press Alt+S or click the book button in the token controls. You can also open the assistant from Configure Settings → Module Settings → Casting Assistant.

The character selector at the top shows the current caster. You can switch to another character you own, or click the person button to open their character sheet. The caster stays selected while you target recipients.

Arrange your window

Resize the window from its corner. Click a panel heading to expand or collapse it. Drag the lower-right corner of an expanded panel’s body to change its height; the body scrolls when its contents no longer fit. Your window size, panel sizes, and open panels are remembered. Use the reset-size button beside the character selector to restore the panel layout. The cost and relevant effect panels open when you choose a spell or profile.

Choose a spell or build

Open Grimoire, choose an entry, and click Prepare cast or Open casting setup. The casting window opens with that selection ready to review. Its list button beside the profile name shows or hides a compact spell and saved-profile browser.

Page references beside the casting ability open through GGA’s PDF links, using the same book mappings as your character sheet. The assistant does not include the books.

Browse your Grimoire

Click Grimoire at the top of the casting assistant, press Alt+B, or use Configure Settings → Module Settings → Grimoire. It opens in a separate window, so you can keep your collection beside the casting assistant.

Find the right entry

Spells shows spells from the selected character’s sheet. Skills & powers shows their skills for power or ritual activation. Saved builds shows all their saved casting profiles, including RPM rituals, powers, standard spells, and threshold setups.

Switch between Cards and List. Search by name, college, class, notes, or PDF reference. Saved builds also search their tags, casting skill, and original pasted text. All search words must match; put a phrase in quotation marks to keep its words together.

View Filters and sorting
Spells / Skills & powers Filter by college or class. Sort by name, skill, lowest listed cost, or college. Variable and unrecognised costs appear last when sorting by cost.
Saved builds Filter by build type or tag. Sort by name, casting skill, base energy, or most recently saved. Older profiles gain a saved date when you next save them.

Click a star to favourite an entry, then use Favourites to show only your favourites. Favourites belong to your user and are separate for each character. Search and filters are remembered separately for each view. Clear resets the current view’s search, filters, and sort.

Inspect and prepare

Select a card or row to see its details, sheet notes, and page references. PDF links on both cards and the detail pane use GGA’s existing book mappings, as on the character sheet. The Grimoire displays your sheet’s information; full spell descriptions remain in your PDFs.

Prepare cast loads the selected spell or skill into the casting assistant. Open casting setup loads a saved build with its chosen settings. Both open the setup for review. Resolve the cast using Cast / Resolve in the assistant. If that window has unsaved changes, you can cancel the replacement.

Organise saved builds

A saved build shows its resource choices, linked attack, recovery, notes, parsed summary, and original build text. Review setup marks missing casting, attack, or resource references that need your attention.

  • Name & tags edits the build’s name and comma-separated tags. Use up to 12 tags. These are saved on the actor and visible to other owners.
  • Duplicate creates a separate copy for a variant. Rename it, then open its casting setup to adjust the energy or effect.
  • Hotbar shortcut creates a macro that opens that saved build.
  • Export downloads the selected build. Export shown downloads only the builds matching the current search and filters.
  • Import adds builds from a Casting Assistant JSON export. Existing builds are retained.
  • Delete removes the selected saved build after confirmation.

Existing profiles and older exports are accepted automatically. New exports require Casting Assistant 0.3.0 or later. Each actor can hold up to 100 saved profiles. When importing builds from another character, review the casting skill, resources, linked attack, and tables before use.

Cast a spell or use a power

  1. Choose a spell or saved build from the Grimoire. Check the Casting spell / skill.
  2. Review the Effect selection and the controls shown for it.
  3. Set the base energy and casting adjustment, and check the resources under Pay with.
  4. For damage, check the linked attack or damage formula. For healing, set HP or FP recovery, the amount, and your targets.
  5. Read the Resolution summary and final energy cost. Set your usual chat roll visibility, then click Cast / Resolve.

The window stays open. The casting summary appears in chat with the roll, outcome, resource changes, and available follow-on buttons.

Choose the effect

Selection What you see
Automatic Uses configured recovery, damage, or a linked attack. A unique matching damage attack can be selected from the sheet. Missile and melee spell classes also show damage controls. Review the resulting setup before casting.
Damage The linked attack, damage formula, and optional attack and damage rolls.
Healing / recovery HP or FP recovery, an amount or dice formula, and target controls.
Other The casting roll and resource cost. Resolve the spell’s other effects in play.

You can override Automatic at any time. Choosing Damage, Healing, or Other preserves the other settings for switching back, but excluded effects do not run. The choice is saved with the profile. A college alone does not establish a spell’s effect; choose a category manually when necessary.

Advanced and combined effects

Open Advanced for casting rules, reduction and failure-cost options, and critical or threshold tables. To use damage and recovery together, choose Damage or Healing and enable Use both damage and recovery controls. Both panels become available. Existing profiles that already contain both effects show both in Automatic mode.

Use Damage options for scaling and Recovery options for self-application. Notes & tags holds the build’s notes and labels. Paste a build opens the RPM parser when you need it.

Resource allocation

FP, HP, and named actor resource trackers are available. Spend down deducts points. Build tally adds points, allowing a tally to exceed its listed threshold. Choose the direction appropriate to each tracker.

Use + Resource to split a cost across pools. For example, a cost of 5 could use 3 Energy Reserve and auto FP for the remaining 2. Only one row may use auto. The allocation must cover the complete cost.

HP and FP spending stops at 0. If your campaign permits spending below 0, handle that cast’s resource changes manually outside the assistant. In Standard spells mode, the assistant adds the casting penalty for the HP you allocate.

Casting rules

Standard spells starts with standard spell-cost settings. Power, RPM profile, and Threshold profile start without standard spell reductions or free critical successes. Adjust the individual options to match the ability and your campaign, then save the profile.

Use the casting-adjustment field for situational modifiers that are not already in GGA or your bucket. Duration, maintenance, resistance rolls, repeated-healing penalties, and special spell restrictions remain for the players and GM to handle.

Save and reuse profiles

Give the setup a useful Profile name, then click Save. A profile stores its casting ability, effect selection, rules options, base cost, resource split, linked attack, damage, recovery, tables, notes, tags, and any parsed build text.

  • Save updates the current saved profile.
  • Save copy creates another profile with its own identity. Rename it for a different energy level or effect.
  • Use Grimoire → Saved builds to browse profiles, or show the quick list and use its Saved tab.
  • The lightning-bolt button creates a shortcut in the Macro Directory. Drag it onto your hotbar. The shortcut opens the saved profile for review.
  • The export button downloads the current profile. Export all downloads every saved profile on the actor.
  • Import adds profiles from a Casting Assistant JSON export. It preserves existing profiles.
  • The bin button deletes the saved profile after confirmation. It does not remove anything from the character sheet.

When transferring a profile to another actor or world, check its casting ability, linked attack, resource trackers, and selected tables. Use the dropdowns to replace missing references. Profiles are saved on the actor; imported sheet spells and skills are not edited.

Parse and save an RPM or power build

For a fresh ritual, click + New RPM build in the Grimoire. The casting assistant opens with an empty RPM setup and the parser panel expanded.

  1. Click Paste a build, or start a + New RPM build from the Grimoire. Paste the text in RPM / power build.
  2. Click Parse build.
  3. Review the detected title, energy cost, damage, and recovery. Enter anything the parser could not identify.
  4. Choose the actual casting skill in Casting spell / skill, such as an appropriate Path skill.
  5. Choose the resource pool or tally and any desired follow-on rolls.
  6. Add useful labels in Notes & tags, then click Save or Save copy.

Return to Grimoire → Saved builds to browse and reuse the result.

The original pasted text and parsed values remain in the profile for later use. Parsing reads the supplied build; it does not calculate an RPM ritual’s energy from its ingredients or create entries on the character sheet. Greater effects and multipliers shown in the preview are informational.

Healing and FP recovery

Choose Healing / recovery if it was not detected automatically. In Healing & recovery, choose HP or FP and enter an amount or formula.

Entry Meaning
4 Restore 4 points of the selected resource.
1d-3! Roll 1d−3, with a minimum result of 1.
/hp +1d-3! Select HP healing and use that formula.
/fp +1d! Select FP recovery and roll 1d.
auto:1 Use the base energy amount.
auto:2 Use twice the base energy amount.

A dice formula is rolled once per successful cast. The resulting amount is used for its recipients, capped at each recipient’s normal maximum.

Apply the effect

Target the recipient tokens, then click Apply recovery to targets on the casting card. Alternatively, enable Apply to my current targets after success before casting. Automatic application uses the recipients targeted when you started that cast.

Each actor can receive a casting card’s recovery once, even if several linked tokens represent that actor. Clicking again does not apply it again to the same actor. You can target a new recipient and use the card where the ability permits multiple recipients.

Players can apply recovery to characters they own. For other characters, an active GM receives a private request and can approve the stated amount and recipients. Without an active GM, only owned recipients can be updated.

Recovery options → Allow the caster as a recipient controls self-application. For standard self-healing, target the caster before casting so the current injury penalty is included in the roll. Recover Energy is handled through resting recovery, not a casting roll.

Undo a mistaken application

A GM can use GM undo beside an applied recipient. Undo is available only while that HP or FP value still matches the value immediately after recovery. If it has changed, adjust the character manually. Undo does not refund casting resources, and the same card cannot reapply the undone effect.

Linked attacks, damage, and tables

Choose Damage if it was not detected automatically. Under Attack & damage, select a linked attack and enable its roll if needed. An Innate Attack skill used for both activation and delivery reuses the same roll. Other linked attacks roll after casting succeeds.

Enter a damage formula, or leave it blank to use the linked attack’s damage. Enable Roll damage after success if desired. Under Damage options, Multiply damage by base energy is for formulas expressed per energy point; leave it off for a complete damage total. A later damage button uses GGA’s normal damage workflow.

Under Advanced → Critical & threshold tables, choose tables from your world. The appropriate buttons appear on the casting card. Set the tally overage per +1 modifier to your campaign’s threshold progression; fractional steps round up.

The casting summary, recovery roll, and linked attack retain the casting roll’s visibility. When rolling damage from an older private card, restore that card’s chat visibility first if prompted.

If something needs attention

Message or symptom What to do
Missing casting ability or attack Select it again in the relevant dropdown, then save the profile.
Resource allocation does not match Check the total at the bottom. Use one auto row for the remainder.
Not enough energy Reduce the cost or split it across available resources.
Resources were not confirmed Read the casting card and any private processing message. Check the actor’s balances before repeating a cast. Follow-on effects are held back when payment is uncertain.
Recovery awaiting GM approval Ask the active GM to use the approval button on the private request.
PDF link does not open Check that the same reference opens on the character sheet and that GGA’s PDF/book mapping is configured.
Damage or recovery controls are missing Check the Effect selector. Choose Damage or Healing, or enable both under Advanced.
A panel is too small Resize its body or use the reset-size button at the top.

Casting Assistant uses the selected component’s statistics and tagged modifiers. It does not run extra check, during, pass, or fail commands stored on the sheet entry. Configure its resource, damage, and recovery operations in the profile.
